About The story behind VEXDYN

Built in Kaduna. Made for builders everywhere.

VEXDYN is a tech and AI company that started with one designer, one laptop and a simple belief: great websites should not be reserved for big budgets.

DA

David Augustine

Founder, VEXDYN

Software engineering studentWeb designer and developerPrivacy · · Kaduna, Nigeria

VEXDYN began as one person building websites. David Augustine, a software engineering student in Kaduna, Nigeria, started out as a freelance web designer and developer, creating sites for local and online clients.

Working under his earlier studio name, DA Noctis Studio, he kept meeting the same problems. Businesses waited weeks for a simple site. Owners could not tell if their pages were fast or easy to find. Brands had no consistent look from one place to the next. So he started building premium website templates and small tools to solve those problems for his own clients first.

The tools kept growing. Forge became the place to write and preview a website. Lab became a place to practice HTML, CSS, JavaScript and React. X-Ray started as a way to check a site's health. Brand-in-a-Box gave new businesses a full identity in one kit. Then came Nyven, an AI assistant for building and for everyday questions, and Agents that anyone can install on their own site.

At that point it was no longer a studio. It was a company, and it needed a name that looked forward. VEXDYN, with the line "Build what's next," became the home for everything, and it is now growing from a creative tech brand into a tech and AI company.

We are still builder first. Everything here is something we needed ourselves, tested on real client work, and designed mobile first because many of our users build from their phones.

Privacy Policy.

Last updated October 19, 2026.

VEXDYN ("we", "us") is a tech and AI company based in Kaduna, Nigeria. This page explains what information this website collects, why, and what choices you have.

What we collect

  • Contact form. If you write to us, we receive your email, your message, and your name if you give it.
  • Visit statistics. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see how many people visit, which pages they open, their country, device type, where they came from, and how fast the site loads. It is built to be privacy-first and to work without tracking you across other sites.
  • Account. If you sign up, we keep your email, your name if you give it, and your password as a one-way hash (we cannot read it). We also keep sign-in records such as the time, IP address and browser, and your email verification and password reset requests.
  • Technical data. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes basic data such as your IP address to deliver the site and keep it secure.

How we use it

We use your message to reply to you. We use account details to let you sign in and to send you messages about your account, like email verification and password reset. We use visit statistics to understand what is working and to improve the site. We do not sell your information.

Who else handles it

  • Cloudflare hosts this website, stores account information in its database service, and provides visit statistics.
  • Brevo sends our account emails, such as email verification and password reset.
  • Resend delivers the contact form message to our inbox.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces on this site, so your browser requests font files from Google.
  • Google provides the optional "Continue with Google" button on the sign-in pages. When you open those pages, your browser loads Google's sign-in script, and if you choose Google we receive your email, name and a Google-verified identity from Google.

Our product sites (Forge, Nyven, X-Ray and Brand-in-a-Box) are separate websites and may have their own privacy policies.

Cookies

This website does not set its own tracking cookies. If you sign in, your browser keeps a sign-in token in its local storage so you stay signed in. Signing out removes it.

How long we keep it

We keep contact messages for as long as we need them to reply and keep a record. We keep account information until you ask us to delete your account. You can ask us to delete yours at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you. Write to us using the contact form on our About page.

Children

This website is not directed at children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
