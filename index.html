About The story behind VEXDYN

Built in Kaduna. Made for builders everywhere.

VEXDYN is a tech and AI company that started with one designer, one laptop and a simple belief: great websites should not be reserved for big budgets.

DA

David Augustine

Founder, VEXDYN

Software engineering studentWeb designer and developerKaduna, Nigeria

VEXDYN began as one person building websites. David Augustine, a software engineering student in Kaduna, Nigeria, started out as a freelance web designer and developer, creating sites for local and online clients.

Working under his earlier studio name, DA Noctis Studio, he kept meeting the same problems. Businesses waited weeks for a simple site. Owners could not tell if their pages were fast or easy to find. Brands had no consistent look from one place to the next. So he started building premium website templates and small tools to solve those problems for his own clients first.

The tools kept growing. Forge became the place to build a website. Lab became a place to practice HTML, CSS, JavaScript and React. X-Ray started as a way to check a site's health. Brand-in-a-Box gave new businesses a full identity in one kit. Then came Nyven, an AI assistant for building and for everyday questions, and Agents that anyone can install on their own site.

At that point it was no longer a studio. It was a company, and it needed a name that looked forward. VEXDYN, with the line "Build what's next," became the home for everything, and it is now growing from a creative tech brand into a tech and AI company.

We are still builder first. Everything here is something we needed ourselves, tested on real client work, and designed mobile first because many of our users build from their phones.
